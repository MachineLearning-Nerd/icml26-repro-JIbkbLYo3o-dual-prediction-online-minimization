# C6 method

The primary verifier checks the corrected construction exactly for symbolic
survivor counts and materializes exact rational rows for `m=1..64`. The proof
is parametric: the rows are regression witnesses, while induction on the
integer survivor count establishes every `m>=1`.

The independent checker reconstructs the harmonic sum without importing the
primary module. The negative control executes the manuscript's printed
remove-before-request order at `m=16`; it must show an exact gap of one.

This is not a claim that the printed proof is correct. It is a repaired,
assumption-preserving proof of the exact lemma, with the defect made visible.
